The science

Light the skin actually uses.

Photobiomodulation delivers photons to the mitochondria, where they drive ATP production and calm oxidative stress. The result is cellular behaviour, not a surface coating: more collagen, less inflammation, slower pigment production.

01

Medical-grade silicone

A 0.4 mm flexible shell wraps the contours of the face so every LED sits within 5 mm of the skin. That proximity is what separates a clinical dose from a decorative one.

02

Eight wavelengths

Red and near-infrared for collagen and deep repair. Blue for blemish bacteria. Green for pigment. Yellow, purple and cyan for radiance, congestion and sensitivity.

03

No consumables

No gels, no refills, no salon appointments. Charge it, wear it for ten minutes, put it back in the pouch. One purchase, years of sessions.
