The science

Light the skin actually uses.

Photobiomodulation delivers photons to the mitochondria, where they drive ATP production and calm oxidative stress. The result is cellular behaviour, not a surface coating: more collagen, less inflammation, slower pigment production.

Our opinion

Power isn't the whole story. Dose is.

Most LED masks advertise more bulbs and more milliwatts. What actually changes skin is the right wavelength reaching the right depth for the right amount of time. Overdose it and you get heat and irritation. Underdose it and nothing happens at all.

The Pro is built around that balance. Veritace® testing checks every single LED against the wavelength used in the published research, and MyDose™ works out the session time from the mode you pick, so you cannot get it wrong.

  • Eight wavelengths, including two invisible near-infrared bands
